# A-106.1

**Assumption.** Memory Leak's tuning in `src/games/memleak/tuning.js` makes a fair, rising challenge: a 10 by 12 heap with a 3-row lane, a fall speed from 1 to 2.4 rows/s (1.8 with reduced motion), a 0.7 to 0.3 s wait after each landing, block lifetimes from 8 to 18 s give or take 30%, a compact meter of 16 freed cells, +1/+3/+6/+10 per freed block by width, +20 per full row, and 4 rows cleared by the Digital Twin.

**Reason.** The issue names the mechanics but not the numbers. A simulated player that drops each block where it lands lowest keeps the heap about a third full at the fastest pace and lasted 400 s on all eight seeds tried, while a player who does nothing overflows in about 80 s. At the fastest pace a block spends 0.83 s in the lane (1.1 s with reduced motion), and a held arrow needs at most 0.41 s to carry it from the middle to a wall.

**Effect if incorrect.** If runs feel too long, raise `LIFE_MAX` or lower `GAP_MIN`: longer lives and shorter waits fill the heap faster. If the late game feels unfair, lower `FALL_CAP` so blocks stay longer in the lane. If compact is rarely ready, lower `COMPACT_CHARGE`.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/games/memleak/fairness.test.js` keeps the lane long enough for the keys and keeps the autopilot alive for 210 s at five sizes. Playtest a few runs on a laptop and a phone for #19, and note when runs end.
