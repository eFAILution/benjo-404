# A-12.1

**Assumption.** The first round of #12 adds packs for the six holidays the issue lists first, using the windows the calendar already has. A pack's window is the days before and after its holiday when the page wears it:
- **New Year's:** 6 days before, 3 after.
- **Valentine's Day:** 14 before, 1 after.
- **St. Patrick's Day:** 10 before, 1 after.
- **Easter:** 14 before, 1 after.
- **Independence Day:** 14 before, 1 after.
- **Thanksgiving:** 14 before, 1 after.

Each pack has its own palette and particles, Benjo with a holiday accessory, two hazards, two pickups, and a short sound cue:

| Pack | Benjo wears | Hazards | Pickups |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's | a party hat | a midnight alarm clock, a firework rocket | a gold star, a party horn |
| Valentine's Day | a headband with two hearts | a broken heart, a stray arrow | a heart, a love letter |
| St. Patrick's Day | a green top hat with a gold buckle | a storm cloud, a black cat | a shamrock, a gold coin |
| Easter | bunny ears | a hatching egg, a rain cloud | a painted egg, a tulip |
| Independence Day | a striped top hat with a star | a firecracker, a rocket | a gold star, a sparkler |
| Thanksgiving | a buckled brown hat | a turkey, an acorn | a slice of pie, a maple leaf |

The lighter days (Groundhog Day, Pi Day, April Fools', Talk Like a Pirate Day) need new calendar entries and come in a later round. Star Wars Day is left out, because its name is a trademark.

**Reason.** The issue asks for these six first, and the calendar already holds them with windows set when the calendar was written (ADR-0008). Bad-luck hazards (a black cat, a storm cloud) and spring ones (a hatching egg, rain) give each holiday things to dodge without making the holiday itself the enemy. Remembrance days stay on the brand theme (A-8.3).

**Effect if incorrect.** A pack's look is one file in `src/themes/<id>/`. A window is two numbers in `src/holidays/calendar.js`. Either can change without touching games or the shell.

**Validation or mitigation.**
- `tests/themes/packs.test.js` checks every pack against the sprite, palette, particle, and size rules, and checks that `benjo.config.json` bundles all of them.
- It checks each new pack on the first day, the day itself, and the last day of its window, and that the pack is gone the day after.
- It checks that Christmas keeps the 26th and New Year's takes the 27th, and that Juneteenth and Veterans Day stay on the brand theme.
- The owner should look at the sprites with `?theme=<id>`. Headless Chromium showed New Year's in Save the File, Valentine's in Uptime Tower, and Thanksgiving picked by `?date=2026-11-20` in Benjo Run, with no console errors.
