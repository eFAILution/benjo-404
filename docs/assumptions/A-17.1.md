# A-17.1

**Assumption.** During a run, the top strip uses two rows under 600px wide (the message on top, the HUD below), not only under 480px. From 600 to 991px the hint text sits on a line just under the strip. Under about 414px a long hint ends in an ellipsis rather than pushing the score off screen.

**Reason.** Return Home ends about 308px from the left. The score group is 215px wide, so the two fit on one row only from about 560px. The hint is centred, so below 992px it reached Return Home (#17). Moving the hint under the strip keeps one row from 600px up. The ellipsis only cuts the hint, which fades after 3 seconds; the score, the pause button, and Return Home stay whole.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the hint must always show in full on phones, the HUD labels ("SCORE", "BEST") could hide under 414px instead. If the strip should stay 56px tall between 480 and 599px, the HUD needs another layout there.

**Validation or mitigation.** Headless Chromium measurements at 360 to 1280px show no overlap and no overflow. Check on a real phone and a tablet.
