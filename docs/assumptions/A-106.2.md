# A-106.2

**Assumption.** On a phone, players can move a Memory Leak block by dragging anywhere on the canvas, a column for every cell the finger slides, and drop it with a tap. A press only counts as a drag after it slides 12 px (`DRAG_PX` in `src/games/memleak/tuning.js`), and a tap drops the block when the finger lifts, not when it touches.

**Reason.** A drag and a tap both start with a press, so the game cannot know which one it is until the finger moves or lifts. A drag measured from where the finger started, and not the block under the finger, works with a thumb that covers the heap, and works on tiny screens where cells are 8 px.

**Effect if incorrect.** If players drop blocks by accident while trying to drag, raise `DRAG_PX`. If dragging feels slow on large screens, scale the drag so one cell of block needs less than one cell of finger travel; that is one line in `src/games/memleak/controls.js`.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/games/memleak/module.test.js` checks that a short press drops, a slide drags and does not drop, and swipes compact and drop. Play it on a phone for #19.
