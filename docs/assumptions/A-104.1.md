# A-104.1

**Assumption.** Crack the Hash's tuning is a fair start: a ring 140 track px in radius, a hit window 30 px either side of the target, targets 140 to 400 px ahead, a start speed of 300 px/s that rises 25 per lock to 450 (380 with reduced motion), locks that need 5 pops and 2 more each lock up to 15, and from lock 2 a 40% chance of a bug kept 80 px from the target (`src/games/crackhash/tuning.js`).

**Reason.** These are guesses from arithmetic, not from play. The window lasts 0.2 s at the start and 0.13 s at top speed, about what one-tap timing games ask for. Lock 1 is over in about 5 s, and eight locks take about a minute for a perfect player, so a run is short but has somewhere to go.

**Effect if incorrect.** If it is too hard, widen `HIT_HALF` or lower `SPEED_CAP`; the fairness test keeps the window at least 0.12 s at top speed. If it is too easy, raise `SPEED_STEP` or `PINS_STEP`, or the bug chance. A wider window needs the bugs kept farther away, which `BUG_SPACE` in `ring.js` does by itself.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/games/crackhash/fairness.test.js` checks that tapping each window's middle opens eight locks at five sizes, that never tapping loses at once, and that no bug comes near a window. Play it on a phone and a laptop for #19.
