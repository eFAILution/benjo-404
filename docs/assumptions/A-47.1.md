# A-47.1

**Assumption.** In Cache Muncher, Digital Twin takes one touch from a chasing cache miss and gives 1.5 seconds in which cache misses pass through Benjo. Garbage Collector sends every cache miss out on the board back to the pen for no points. Magnet munches the bytes within two cells of Benjo along the open paths, never through a wall, and leaves the Clear cache pellets alone. In Broken Links, `host.track` follows the cursor, because the cursor is what the player steers and Benjo stands still beside the board.

**Reason.** #22 asks for power-ups and the ghost race in every game, and ADR-0022 §8 says the ghost needs only `track`. Without these, Cache Muncher got only Cache Hit and Breakpoint, and Whack-a-Bug and Broken Links drew no ghost or twin. Pellets are left out of the magnet because when to clear the cache is the game's main choice. Munching the cache misses for points would make Garbage Collector worth more here than in any other game.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the magnet clears a level too fast, it can drop to one cell or skip bytes that Benjo is heading away from. If a ghost cursor confuses Broken Links players, the game can stop calling `track`, as ADR-0022 §8 allows for games without an avatar, and lose the ghost race.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/games/muncher/powers.test.js` covers the twin, the magnet (including a byte behind a wall), the collector, rollback and `track`. The `track` tests in `tests/games/whack/powers.test.js` and `tests/games/links/powers.test.js` cover the other two games. Play-test the magnet's reach on a phone.
