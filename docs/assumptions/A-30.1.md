# A-30.1

**Assumption.** Level 1 of Cache Muncher is fair to a first-time visitor with these settings:

- Cache misses move at 0.8 of Benjo's speed.
- Three of them leave the pen at 3, 8, and 14 s, after a 1 s ready pause.
- A Clear cache pellet makes them catchable for 6 s.

Later levels get harder: cache misses speed up to 0.95 of Benjo, both speeds rise 5% a level, and the catchable time drops to at least 2 s.

**Reason.** The arcade's level 1 ghosts are close to Pac-Man's speed, but people land on a 404 page by accident, with no warm-up. The fairness autopilot is a simple path-to-the-nearest-byte bot with a flee move. At a ratio of 0.85, or with releases at 2, 6, and 10 s, it ran out of lives on some seeds. At 0.8 with releases at 3, 8, and 14 s, it cleared level 1 on all eight seeds, always with a life left.

**Effect if incorrect.** If it plays too easy, the ratio can go back toward 0.85. If it plays too hard, the release times can grow, or the catchable time can be longer. The autopilot is the guard rail for either change.

**Validation or mitigation.** The fairness test runs at two sizes, with and without reduced motion. Play-test a few levels on a phone and a desktop.
