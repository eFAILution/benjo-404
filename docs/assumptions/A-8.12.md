# A-8.12

**Assumption.** On touch, Benjo Run jumps on the pointer down edge and keeps the jump high while the finger is held. The host's `primary` tap that follows on pointer up is dropped as an echo.

**Reason.** The host only reports a tap as `primary` on pointer up, up to 300 ms after the press. A jump that late feels broken in a reaction game.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the host later reports presses on pointer down, the game can go back to `primary` alone and drop its own pointer tracking in `src/games/runner/controls.js`.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play on a phone and check that a short tap gives a short hop and a long press a high jump.
