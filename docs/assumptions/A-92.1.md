# A-92.1

**Assumption.** Save the File is fair and fun with these numbers:
- **Bugs:** a throw every 2.6 s, give or take 30%. Bugs roll at 62 units/s, take 25% of the ladders they cross, and never take one while another bug is within 72 units of its foot.
- **Benjo:** walks at 72 units/s, climbs at 54, and jumps at 250 against gravity of 700. His hitbox is 12 by 22 units, and a bug's is a circle of radius 7.
- **Clock:** 60 s to save the file.
- **Each level:** bugs 10% faster, throws 0.25 s sooner, and ladders 8% likelier, up to limits.

**Reason.** These are the numbers that let a careful keyboard autopilot save level 1 with every life and save three files without a game over, on eight seeds. A Benjo who just walks to the first ladder and waits is still hit within a minute. Earlier numbers failed in two ways. A jump in place could barely clear a slow bug. And bugs coming down a ladder in front of another bug landed in pairs too tight to jump.

**Effect if incorrect.** If it plays too hard, lower `BUG_SPEED` or `LADDER_CHANCE`, or raise `THROW_T` or `TIME_START` in `src/games/savefile/tuning.js`. If it's too easy, do the opposite. Any change must keep `tests/games/savefile/fairness.test.js` passing.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play it on a desktop and a phone for #19, and check that swipe-to-climb and hold-to-walk feel right on touch. Headless Chromium at 1280x800 and at 390x844 with touch drew the level, the bugs and Benjo, with no console errors.
