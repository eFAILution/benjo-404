# A-22.2

**Assumption.** A visitor reaches v1.0.0, and the first Benjo look, in roughly ten to fifteen runs. The first power-up drops about 10 seconds into a run, then one every 15 to 25 seconds.

**Reason.** Most visitors play a handful of short runs. An unlock they never reach does nothing, and a drop that takes a minute misses most runs.

**Effect if incorrect.** If unlocks feel too fast or too slow, the level cost (40 + 10L) in `src/shell/progress.js` and the drop timing in `src/shell/power.js` are each one or two numbers.

**Validation or mitigation.** Watch real players during the device check in #19 and tune the numbers.
