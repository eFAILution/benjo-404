# A-100.1

**Assumption.** Tower of Benjo is fair and fun with these numbers:
- **Floors:** 11 x 9 mazes with 14 loops, and a 60 s clock per floor.
- **Benjo:** walks at 3.2 cells/s. His sword covers 60° either side of the way he faces, walking or standing.
- **Enemies:** slimes at 1.3 cells/s and knights at 1.7, 5% faster per floor up to 1.5 times. Mages show 2 to 6 cells away in a straight line and cast a bolt at 5 cells/s.
- **Roster:** grows every two to four floors, up to 6 slimes, 3 knights, and 2 mages.

**Reason.** With these numbers, a keyboard autopilot with a one-second lookahead clears three floors without a game over on every seed tried, and a Benjo who stands still loses every life. Without the lookahead, the simple path-and-face heuristic cleared two to five floors but lost lives to enemies arriving from side corridors. That led to the sword counting while Benjo stands as well as walks.

**Effect if incorrect.** If it plays too hard, lower `KNIGHT_SPEED` or `SPEED_GROWTH`, raise `FLOOR_T`, or slow the roster in `rosterFor`. If it's too easy, do the opposite. Any change must keep `tests/games/benjotower/fairness.test.js` passing.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play it on a desktop and a phone for #19, and check that hold-to-walk and lift-to-shield feel right on touch. On a phone held upright the maze is wide and short, with empty space above and below. Headless Chromium at 1280x800 and at 390x844 with touch drew the maze, key, door, enemies, and Benjo with his sword and shield, with no console errors.
