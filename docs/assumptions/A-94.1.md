# A-94.1

**Assumption.** On a phone, one held finger is enough to play Bug Belt. Holding turns the ship's nose toward the finger and fires. When the finger is more than 90 px from the ship (`THRUST_NEAR` in `src/games/bugbelt/tuning.js`) and the nose points its way, the ship also thrusts there.

**Reason.** The arcade game needs turn, thrust and fire at once. On-screen buttons for all three crowd a small screen and hide the bugs under the player's thumbs. Every other game here plays with one finger, and players expect that.

**Effect if incorrect.** If players find it hard to stop or to aim without moving, raise `THRUST_NEAR` so more of the screen only aims. If they want to fire less, fire only on a tap and not while held; that is one line in `src/games/bugbelt/controls.js`.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/games/bugbelt/world.test.js` checks that a far finger flies the ship and a near one only aims. Play it on a phone for #19.
