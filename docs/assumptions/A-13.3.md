# A-13.3

**Assumption.** A pickup is worth two points, a tower one, and the score label is "SCORE". The best score is kept per game, under the `flight` storage namespace.

**Reason.** Pickups sit off the line between two gaps, so reaching one is a small risk worth more than a gap. Separate bests per game avoid comparing distance with gaps.

**Effect if incorrect.** `PICKUP_SCORE` in `src/games/flight/tuning.js` changes. Nothing else does.

**Validation or mitigation.** Playtest a few runs and check that pickups feel worth chasing without being required.
