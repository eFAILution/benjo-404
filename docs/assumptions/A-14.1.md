# A-14.1

**Assumption.** Option 1 (random start plus a picker) is the default until the team decides. The other two options are settings in `benjo.config.json` (`startGame`, `gamePicker`), not separate builds.

**Reason.** The owner asked for option 1 for now, with the final call left to the other developers. A setting makes that call a one-line change.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the team picks option 2 or 3, change the two fields as the table in ADR-0014 shows. If the team wants the unused options gone, delete the fields and `pickGame`'s `first` branch.

**Validation or mitigation.** The team reviews ADR-0014 and sets its status to Accepted with the chosen option.

**Status.** open
