# A-22.3

**Assumption.** The nightly build, its streak, and the Legacy Code trophy all count days in UTC. A ghost is saved whenever a run beats the best, including the very first run of a game.

**Reason.** UTC is the only way everyone gets the same nightly on the same day. Saving the first run's ghost means a player always has a past self to race.

**Effect if incorrect.** Visitors far from UTC see the nightly change in the middle of their day. If that confuses people, the day could follow the visitor's clock instead, as holidays do (A-8.3), at the cost of different players getting different runs.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/shell/nightly.test.js` and `tests/shell/ghost.test.js` cover the day math and ghost saving.
