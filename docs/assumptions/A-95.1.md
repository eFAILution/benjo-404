# A-95.1

**Assumption.** Uptime Tower is fair and fun with these numbers:
- **Jump:** 640 units/s from a standstill, plus 0.75 of the running speed, against gravity of 1800.
- **Run:** up to 300 units/s.
- **Floors:** 72 units apart, narrowing from about 170 to 70, each within 140 of the one below.
- **View:** starts climbing at floor 5, at 30 units/s, speeding up by 8 every 15 s to 110 (85 with reduced motion).
- **Bugs:** from floor 8, on 15% of floors rising to 45%, crawling at 40 units/s and up.
- **Hitboxes:** Benjo's is 10 units wide and a bug's 9.

**Reason.** With these numbers, a keyboard autopilot with a one-second lookahead climbs to floor 40 alive on every seed and size tried. A standing jump clears a bug crawling at Benjo with about 0.19 s to spare at the slowest bug speed, so the jump-over a player tries first works. Earlier numbers (a 560 or 600 jump and wider hitboxes) left a window of only 0.07 s or none.

**Effect if incorrect.** If it plays too hard, lower `SCROLL_CAP`, `BUG_MAX` or `BUG_STEP`, or raise `FLOOR_W_MIN`. If it's too easy, do the opposite. Each is one constant in `src/games/tower/tuning.js`, and any change must keep `tests/games/tower/fairness.test.js` passing.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play it on a desktop and a phone for #19, and check that press-to-jump and hold-to-run feel right on touch. Headless Chromium at 1280x800 and at 390x844 with touch drew the tower, floors, bugs and Benjo, scored, and threw no console errors.
