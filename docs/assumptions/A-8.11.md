# A-8.11

**Assumption.** When Benjo is hit, Benjo Run holds for 0.4 seconds with a flash and a screen shake before the game over card. There is no shake under reduced motion.

**Reason.** Plan section 9 ends the run on the hit. Without a pause the player cannot see what got them.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the owner wants the card at once, the hit stop constant in `src/games/runner/tuning.js` becomes 0.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play with and without OS reduced motion and confirm the pause, the flash, and no shake when reduced.
