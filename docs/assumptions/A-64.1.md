# A-64.1

**Assumption.** A visitor whose primary pointer is coarse (`(pointer: coarse)`) plays with touch, and anyone else plays with keys. Once the visitor gives any input, the last one wins: a touch or pen pointer means touch, and a mouse pointer or any key means keys. The How to play card and a run's first HUD hint show only the controls for that guess.

**Reason.** Phones and tablets report a coarse primary pointer, and computers with a mouse or trackpad report a fine one. The card often shows before any input, when the guess is all there is, and the tap or key that opens it already corrects a wrong guess.

**Effect if incorrect.** A laptop with a touch screen that is played by touch shows keys on the first card, until its first tap. A tablet with a keyboard shows touch until its first key. Either visitor can reopen How to play from the start screen or the paused card, and it then follows the last input.

**Validation or mitigation.** `tests/shell/device.test.js` covers the guess and the switches. Headless Chromium at 390x844 with touch emulation showed only the Touch lines and "Tap the slot to fire it", and at 1280x800 with a mouse only the Keys lines and "Press E to fire it". Check both on real devices in #19.
