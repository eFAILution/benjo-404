# A-8.9

**Assumption.** On screens under 960px wide, Benjo Run plays slower through a `pace` factor from 0.7 to 1. Speeds are multiplied by `pace` and gravity by `pace` squared. The base speeds and gravity in `src/games/runner/tuning.js` apply exactly at 960px and wider.

**Reason.** The base speeds are in pixels per second. On a phone the same speed crosses the screen in far less time, which leaves too little reaction time. Scaling gravity by the square keeps the jump arc the same shape.

**Effect if incorrect.** If phone play should match desktop timing, `PACE_MIN` in `src/games/runner/tuning.js` becomes 1.

**Validation or mitigation.** Play on a phone and on desktop and compare how fair the first 30 seconds feel.
