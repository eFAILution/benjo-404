# A-28.1

**Assumption.** The How to play card shows once per game per browser: the first time play is pressed for a game the visitor has not seen. "Seen" is stored in `localStorage` under the shell's `howto` namespace, and a visitor can reopen the card with How to play on the start screen or the paused card. Opening it from either place, then pressing Got it, play or Resume, marks it seen. Back does not.

**Reason.** Visitors land on a 404 page by accident and have never seen these games. Once they have read a game's card, showing it on every run would slow them down. The best score already uses `localStorage` the same way.

**Effect if incorrect.** If the owner wants the card on every first run of a page visit instead, the flag could move to memory. If players skip the card without reading it, the paused card's How to play button is the fallback.

**Validation or mitigation.** In headless Chromium, the first play showed the card. Got it, play started the run. On the paused card, How to play opened the card, and Back returned to the paused card. After a reload, play started straight away. Play-test it with someone who has not seen the games.
